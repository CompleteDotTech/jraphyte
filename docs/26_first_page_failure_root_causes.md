# Root causes in the 200-paper first-page comparison

The six pipelines received the same 200 physical first pages. The assistant-reviewed source references contain 111 complete abstracts, 7 abstracts that continue beyond page one, and 82 pages without an abstract. These are diagnostic references; independent human review is outstanding. A missing proposal on a page without a complete first-page abstract is a correct abstention.

The table separates failures to propose a complete abstract from incorrect proposals. Each row's first three numeric columns sum to 111; the last column counts proposals on the 7 partial or 82 no-abstract pages. A correct proposal requires at least 98% ordered canonical character precision and recall against the complete source reference.

| Pipeline | Correct complete proposals | Complete abstracts withheld | Complete abstracts proposed incorrectly | Partial/absent pages proposed |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Production baseline | 77 | 13 | 21 | 2 |
| Docling + geometry v1 | 80 | 19 | 12 | 7 |
| GROBID | 92 | 13 | 6 | 15 |
| Docling + structure v2 | 91 | 16 | 4 | 1 |
| MinerU + structure v2 | 77 | 30 | 4 | 2 |
| olmOCR + structure v2 | 41 | 69 | 1 | 1 |

## What caused the differences

1. **First-page scope and genuine absences.** Many supplied PDFs start with a cover, contents, highlights, key points, author details, or an image. The paper may contain an abstract later, but the test does not use later pages. The seven partial abstracts remain partial even when a parser returns their visible first-page text. For example, f199 has a title and long author list but no abstract on page one. The f151 Executive Summary is classified as no separate abstract; that boundary choice is disclosed in the [expanded results](../review/first_page_expanded200/results.json).
2. **Production baseline: PDF block boundaries.** Its direct PyMuPDF extraction uses heading, length, punctuation, and block-layout rules. Among its 21 incorrect proposals on complete abstracts, 20 contain extra text by the scoring criterion and one omits text. It also misses 13 complete abstracts. A sentence ending or nearby block is not a reliable abstract boundary: f030 incorporates Overview text, and f192 incorporates a dedication.
3. **Geometry v1: page coordinates do not always give reading order.** It sorts Docling regions geometrically and uses column overlap and section headings to stop. Ten of its 12 incorrect proposals on complete abstracts include extra text; f150 is a wrong or mixed-column selection. It proposes text on seven partial or absent pages. Six complete references are recovered textually but held because the selected text lacks the expected final sentence punctuation.
4. **GROBID: header-field classification does not enforce the visual abstract boundary.** It proposed 15 partial or absent pages: six partial abstracts and nine no-abstract pages. The latter include highlights/key points (for example f009 and f152) and a book-jacket-like image at f185. Of its six wrong proposals on complete abstracts, three include extra text, two omit text, and one is a wrong or mixed region (f104 selects figure-caption material). Thirteen complete abstracts have no GROBID proposal. One HTTP 500 occurred on f076, an image-only cover labeled no abstract; this did not account for a missed complete abstract.
5. **Structure v2: remaining semantic boundaries and cautious holds.** Its five wrong proposals are f030 (Overview), f119 (separate colored synopsis), f150 (right-column body in place of the left-column bold abstract), f192 (dedication), and f199 (author details mistaken for an unlabelled abstract). The [visual audit](../review/first_page_expanded200/visual_audit.json) binds the four new cases to page-image and assessment hashes. Of 16 complete abstracts withheld, eight actually match the source at the 98% text threshold: four fail the final-sentence check, three fail native-versus-layout agreement, and one reaches the page bottom without a trusted closing boundary. The other eight do not recover matching text. Some holds also exceed the 4,000-character request budget; reason counts overlap.
6. **MinerU: correct conversion is not correct selection.** All 200 pages converted, but 30 complete abstracts were withheld; 19 of these have a native-versus-model disagreement reason. Six withheld abstracts nevertheless meet the text-match threshold. Its false proposals include affiliations treated as unlabelled abstracts (f041 and f136), plus abstract text extended into other page material (f119, f128, f150, f192). The MinerU fallback adds one correct proposal and three wrong proposals where structure v2 abstains.
7. **olmOCR: the native-alignment gate limits acceptance.** The local adapter estimates OCR paragraph boxes by matching them to native PDF lines because the model does not supply source coordinates. Of 69 complete abstracts withheld, 41 have text that meets the source-match threshold; 39 of those 41 are held for native-versus-model disagreement and two for the final-sentence check. The remaining 28 withheld abstracts do not match the reference. Its two false proposals are f068 (author note) and f119 (colored synopsis). Its fallback adds no correct proposal and one wrong proposal where structure v2 abstains. Three outputs (f061, f072, f095) reached the 4,096-token *generation* cap and were withheld; all three source pages are labeled no abstract. This is separate from the 4,000-character request budget and from any 30,000-token API limit.

The shared selector calls a region complete after a plausible start, stop and final sentence; a separate agreement check can withhold it. Visual features such as bold type, color, and column membership are not fully represented by the current text and region labels. The failures above are observed for these frozen configurations; they do not measure standalone OCR accuracy or establish a safe automatic admission threshold.

## Retrieval failures

The local reranker put the labeled target first for 57 of 60 queries. It put f020 second; because only one relevant paper per query was judged, the top-ranked competing paper has not been independently judged irrelevant. The other two misses are candidate-generation failures: f076 has no usable local title, abstract, or body text, while f115's automatic title is `Graphical Abstract` and its actual title is only in the body field. Neither target entered the top-50 BM25F/SPECTER2 candidate union, so the reranker could not recover it. See the [retrieval failure receipts](../review/first_page_expanded200/retrieval_failures.json).

The [200-paper results](../review/first_page_expanded200/results.json), frozen reference hashes, assessment receipts, and parser outputs support these counts. Source PDFs and rendered pages remain in the adjacent private test directory. No selector was tuned on the added 100, and this audit made no API calls or graph writes.
